GTA Clips Uploader

GTA Clips Uploader is a personal desktop tool. It cuts short vertical highlight clips from gaming streams and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. The owner reviews every draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to post it.

The tool is used only by its owner and only with TikTok accounts the owner controls.
