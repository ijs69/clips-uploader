Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What the app accesses

With the account holder's permission, the app uses the TikTok scopes user.info.basic and video.upload: it reads basic profile information and uploads video drafts to that account.

Storage

The access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored only on the owner's computer. The app has no server, does not collect analytics, and does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Deletion

The account holder can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings under Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless; the owner can also delete them from the computer.
